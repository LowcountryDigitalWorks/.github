# Role Model

## Owner

Reserved for people who must administer organization membership, security,
applications, and repository governance.

## Member

Used for regular organization participants. Repository permissions should be
granted by team or direct role according to need.

## Outside collaborator

Preferred for temporary or narrowly scoped access when organization membership is
unnecessary.

## Current transition

Multiple owner identities may remain temporarily during setup. Long-term access
should be reduced after recovery, deployment, and handoff procedures are tested.
