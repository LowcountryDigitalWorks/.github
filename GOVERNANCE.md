# Organization Governance

## Ownership

Company-owned repositories belong to the `LowcountryDigitalWorks` organization.
Individual accounts receive named access and should not become the durable owner of
company assets.

Client repositories should ordinarily be client-owned, with Lowcountry Digital
Works granted named, role-based access.

## Authority

Eddie Gugino is the primary business representative and final approver for:

- purchases and recurring costs;
- account ownership and recovery changes;
- DNS, domain, email, deployment, and billing changes;
- public claims and permanent brand decisions;
- client-data processing and third-party integrations;
- production releases with material risk.

## Change control

Meaningful changes use branches and pull requests. Consequential changes require a
documented current state, proposed state, validation, rollback, and explicit
approval.
