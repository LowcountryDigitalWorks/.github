# Lowcountry Digital Works

Lowcountry Digital Works is a small digital services business based in the South
Carolina Lowcountry. Work may include practical websites, web and offline
applications, workflow automation, technical problem-solving, account and platform
transitions, maintenance, and related digital systems.

## Working principles

- Keep the client in control of business assets.
- Prefer named, role-based access instead of shared credentials.
- Use maintainable and portable systems.
- Avoid unnecessary subscriptions and infrastructure.
- Document deployment, ownership, recovery, and handoff.
- Test changes before release.
